# Dataedo selection and scope

Checked 14 October 2026. New data-catalog family; the registry has no previous built target in this family. Tonight's strategic-sourcing and credit-control families were excluded.

The [vendor pricing page](https://dataedo.com/pricing) shows annual USD prices of 18,000 for Essentials, 24,000 for Data Lineage and 32,000 for Data Quality, at the default three-editor minimum. Viewers and integrations are unlimited. This is a published price, not an observed NZ or AU customer invoice. No savings percentage is asserted. Dataedo itself supports on-premises deployment and exports, so the comparison must not claim its users do not own their data.

The [Select Star pricing route](https://www.selectstar.com/pricing) redirects to its product home page. [Secoda pricing](https://www.secoda.co/pricing) describes plans but gives no paid dollar price. Their annual bill scores are unverified-price penalties, not estimates of their fees. These are candidate judgments, not market statistics. Alternative-search results from G2 and Capterra establish comparison intent, not measured demand or traffic. No named customer spend was verified in the available local customer and partner records, so buyer fit is conservative. No Dataedo resale relationship was found in those records.

Dataedo [plans documentation](https://docs.dataedo.com/intro/plans/) and [Excel export instructions](https://docs.dataedo.com/data-catalog/building-catalog/sharing/exporting-documentation/excel-export-portal/) support the catalogue scope and export route. Its [stored-data description](https://docs.dataedo.com/intro/what-is-stored/) distinguishes metadata from profiling and quality samples. This free build stores metadata and operator-entered quality evidence only. Automatic connectors, source scanning, automatic lineage, profiling and portal access controls require separate implementation.

No claim is made that Dataedo cannot answer the ten example questions. Their value here is combining the business's own review rules, recorded dependencies and evidence in editable queries. All demo names and observations are synthetic. Imported workbook layouts must be checked against the actual selected export; the included fixtures demonstrate mappings and do not pretend to come from a customer.
